03 Featured · Capstone

Final-year thesis (TCC) · Control & Automation, POLI-UPE · approved 2026

CrossPack — automatic barcode validation to prevent cross-packing

CompactLogixStudio 5000 · LadderEthernet/IP · CIPHMI · ISA-101EAN barcodeIEC 61131-3

Cross-packing — product sealed into the wrong packaging — is an operator-origin failure that causes financial loss and breaks batch traceability. My system is a three-tier design: an industrial barcode reader → PLC → HMI, networked over Ethernet/IP (CIP), continuously comparing each package's code against the recipe the operator selects on the HMI.

On any mismatch it raises tiered alarms and automatically stops the filler machine, so mis-packed product never advances down the line.

  • Readers (Keyence SR-X100): configured for high-speed lines via static-IP Ethernet in "moving codes" / continuous-trigger mode.
  • PLC (Ladder / Studio 5000): a custom Receita_SKU data type plus routines for reader comms, ASCII→STRING conversion, sequential recipe lookup, and tiered alarms — timeout, divergence and communication failure.
  • HMI (ISA-101): four new screens (operation, recipe selection, editing, parameters) letting operators manage up to 45 recipes.
  • Validated in a real consumer-goods plant over a Dec 2025–Feb 2026 observation window.
